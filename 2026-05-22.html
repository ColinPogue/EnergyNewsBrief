Top Stories
Iran Diplomacy Stalls as Hormuz Fears Drive WTI to $105
OilPrice / CNBC / Upstream Online
July WTI crude surged to a weekly high of $105.21 before retreating to $97.77 as traders aggressively priced in geopolitical risk around the US-Iran conflict, then partially unwound that premium on diplomatic signals. Secretary of State Rubio cited 'good signs' toward a deal, but core sticking points — enriched uranium and Iranian-imposed Strait of Hormuz tolls — remain unresolved. Analysts warn global oil inventories are declining rapidly, with fuel prices potentially spiking sharply within four to six weeks if the conflict persists.
Qatari LNG Outage Set to Tip Global Gas Market Into Deficit
Rigzone
Enverus Intelligence Research warns that the ongoing Qatari LNG outage will push the global natural gas market into a structural supply deficit, a significant shift for an already tight market. The disruption adds urgency for buyers reliant on Qatari volumes to secure alternative supply, with spot LNG prices likely to face sustained upward pressure. Traders should watch for rerouting activity and premium contract negotiations as the outage duration remains uncertain.
Walmart Warns Iran War Squeezing Consumers at the Pump
Financial Times
Walmart reported that customers are rationing fuel purchases as the Iran conflict drives US gasoline prices to their highest levels in nearly four years, prompting the retailer to absorb higher fuel costs to shield consumers. The retail giant's shares fell sharply following the disclosure, underscoring how energy price stress is transmitting rapidly into consumer behavior and corporate earnings. The development signals mounting demand-side risk for refined products if prices remain elevated heading into peak summer driving season.
Diversified Energy Pays $1.175B for Anadarko Basin Gas Assets
NGI
Diversified Energy has agreed to acquire Anadarko Basin oil and natural gas assets from Camino Natural Resources for $1.175 billion, marking its third Oklahoma deal since 2025 and signaling renewed institutional confidence in the basin. The acquisition deepens Diversified's exposure to US natural gas production at a time when domestic gas prices are elevated and export demand from Mexico is accelerating due to summer cooling load. The deal reflects a broader trend of buyers returning to the Anadarko as gas fundamentals improve.

Market Outlook
The energy complex is caught between two high-conviction opposing forces: a partial diplomatic thaw on Iran and structural supply deficits building across both crude and LNG. WTI's $105.21 intraday spike and subsequent retreat to $96.52 illustrates just how hair-trigger this market is to headline risk — Rubio's 'good signs' language was enough to drain roughly $7-8 of premium in hours, but with enriched uranium sticking points and Hormuz toll demands unresolved, that premium is not gone, it is simply parked. Analysts flagging a potential sharp fuel price spike within four to six weeks if conflict persists aligns with the Walmart disclosure that US consumers are already rationing fuel purchases at pump prices near four-year highs — a demand destruction signal that traders must monitor closely as it could cap upside in refined products even if crude stays elevated. On gas, the Qatari LNG outage is a market-moving event that has not yet been fully priced at $3.07; spot LNG rerouting premiums and Mexico summer cooling demand layered onto the Diversified-Anadarko deal thesis create a constructive setup, and $3.15-$3.20 is the near-term technical trigger level. The WTI futures curve's sharp backwardation — $96.52 front month collapsing to $86.59 by Oct 2026 — confirms the market is treating current tightness as event-driven rather than cyclical, which means any Iran resolution trades sharply lower and any escalation trades sharply higher with asymmetric velocity. Key catalysts to watch: formal Iran deal announcement or breakdown, Qatari LNG outage duration update, next EIA inventory print, and any Hormuz shipping incident that would immediately invalidate the diplomatic premium unwind.
