Top Stories
Trump Extends Iran Blockade Threat, Oil Prices Surge to Multi-Week Highs
CNBC / Upstream Online
Crude oil prices climbed to their highest levels in weeks after reports emerged that the Trump administration is preparing to extend its blockade of Iranian ports, compounding supply fears already elevated by the ongoing Strait of Hormuz closure. President Trump further escalated tensions with a threatening TruthSocial post directed at Iran, rattling markets and reinforcing upside price momentum. Traders are closely watching for any signals of diplomatic resolution, with Brent and WTI both responding sharply to each new development.
UAE Quits OPEC Effective May 1; Russia Pledges to Stay
OilPrice / CNBC / World Oil
The United Arab Emirates announced it is exiting OPEC and the broader OPEC+ alliance effective May 1, citing national interest in pursuing unconstrained production growth — a move Barclays analysts say will accelerate UAE supply expansion once the Hormuz crisis subsides. The departure is raising questions about who else might follow, with Saudi Arabia and other members facing fresh cohesion challenges. Russia, meanwhile, confirmed it will remain within OPEC+, seeking to project alliance stability amid the shock of the UAE exit.
TotalEnergies Middle East Output Offline Indefinitely Amid Conflict
NGI
TotalEnergies confirmed that approximately 15% of its Middle East oil and natural gas production remains shut in with no timeline for restart, as the regional conflict shows no sign of resolution. The outage underscores how the broader supply shock is affecting major international operators beyond national oil companies, with material volumes removed from the market. For crude and gas traders, the open-ended nature of the disruption adds persistent uncertainty to forward supply balances.
Alaska proved reserves increased in 2024, while nationwide proved reserves fell
EIA
Oil and gas producers operating in Alaska reported increases in proved reserves in 2024 at a time when low prices triggered a decrease in nationwide proved reserves, according to our recently released U.S. Crude Oil and Natural Gas Proved Reserves, Year-End 2024 report.
