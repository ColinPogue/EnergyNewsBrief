Top Stories
Iran Talks Collapse, Hormuz Traffic Stalls, Oil Prices Surge
CNBC / OilPrice / Rigzone / Upstream Online
U.S.-Iran peace negotiations hit an immediate wall after Swiss talks failed to proceed, with Vice President Vance canceling his Geneva trip over unresolved logistical issues; Iran separately delayed permanent peace talks following intensified fighting in southern Lebanon. Tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz ground to a near halt Friday morning, with no outbound vessels recorded, reversing a brief post-framework revival. Oil prices swung sharply on the developments, underscoring how fragile any Hormuz shipping recovery remains and how closely crude markets are tracking diplomatic progress.
Natural Gas Futures Climb as Storage Deficit Widens
NGI
July natural gas futures settled higher Thursday after the EIA storage report showed injections continuing to lag year-earlier levels, extending a season-long deficit trend that is tightening the supply cushion. Meanwhile, cash prices pulled back for the Juneteenth holiday weekend on softer power demand forecasts, and an Alliance Pipeline outage drew market attention as a near-term supply risk. Permian Basin forward prices rebounded sharply over the June 10–17 period even as New England winter strip prices retreated, reflecting diverging regional supply-demand dynamics.
OPEC Demand Forecasts Dismissed as Wishful Thinking by FT
Financial Times
The Financial Times is challenging OPEC's long-term oil demand projections, characterizing the cartel's outlook as overly optimistic and disconnected from accelerating energy transition trends. For traders, the credibility gap between OPEC's supply management assumptions and independent demand forecasts adds uncertainty to medium-term price discovery and production strategy signals.
Venture Global Locks In 5-Year LNG Supply Deal with Germany's EnBW
Rigzone
Venture Global has executed agreements to supply approximately 0.82 million metric tons per annum of U.S. LNG to German utility EnBW under a five-year contract, adding to its growing European customer base. The deal reinforces the ongoing structural shift of U.S. LNG exports toward European buyers seeking to diversify away from Russian gas supplies.
Permian natural gas production increased faster than crude oil
EIA
The Permian region's marketed natural gas production grew from 17.2 billion cubic feet per day (Bcf/d) in 2021 to 27.6 Bcf/d in 2025, a 60% increase, according to data from our latest Short Term Energy Outlook. Over the same period, crude oil production grew by 39%, going from 4.7 million barrels per day (b/d) to 6.6 million b/d.
Market Outlook
The crude market is firmly back in geopolitical risk mode, with Brent breaking above $80.50 and the Hormuz disruption narrative front and center after zero outbound tanker movements were recorded Friday morning — a stark reversal of the brief post-framework normalization. WTI's near-flat session despite the Brent surge highlights a bifurcated market: waterborne supply risk is being priced into Brent, while robust Permian output growth (crude up 39% to 6.6 MMbbl/d since 2021) and a bearish WTI futures curve rolling from $77.35 in Jul 2026 to $73.98 in Nov 2026 keep domestic prices anchored. The collapse of Swiss-brokered Iran talks is the dominant near-term catalyst — traders should price in a multi-day minimum for any diplomatic re-engagement, meaning Hormuz risk premium is unlikely to evaporate quickly. On the demand side, the FT's challenge to OPEC's long-term demand projections adds medium-term noise to price discovery, particularly as OPEC+ supply management decisions increasingly rely on contested demand assumptions; this credibility gap argues for wider risk ranges on 2026 price targets. Natural gas faces its own crosscurrent: the widening EIA storage deficit is constructive for $NGH, but Permian associated gas flooding the grid at 27.6 Bcf/d and the Venture Global-EnBW LNG deal locking in 0.82 MMTPA of export capacity signal that supply is scaling structurally to meet any demand upside. Key events to watch: weekend Hormuz traffic reports, any diplomatic back-channel signals from Tehran or Washington, Tuesday's post-holiday cash gas markets reopening, and weekly EIA storage data for confirmation of the deficit trend.
